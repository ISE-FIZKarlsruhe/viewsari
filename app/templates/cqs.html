Competency Questions

Competency questions are the real-world questions the Viewsari ontology and knowledge graph were built to answer. They were collected from interviews with four kinds of users — from art historians to software engineers — and turned into a checklist that both guides the ontology's design and tests how well it covers the material in Vasari's Lives. Many can be answered directly as a SPARQL query against the graph.
