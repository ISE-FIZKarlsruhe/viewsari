# Publications

This file lists peer-reviewed publications associated with the Viewsari project. Parts of the research presented in the dissertation previously appeared in preliminary form in workshops, conference contributions, and doctoral presentations. They document intermediate stages of conceptual development; the dissertation substantially extends and integrates these results into a coherent methodological framework.

---
