artworkc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001012

A creative work — painting, sculpture, fresco, architectural project, or decorative object — mentioned or described in the Vasari corpus. Artwork individuals are the body values of viewsari:explicit_mention and viewsari:implicit_mention instances. Where a Wikidata entity exists for the artwork, owl:sameAs links the Viewsari individual to the corresponding QID. Where no Wikidata entity exists (the out-of-knowledge-base case), the individual receives a Viewsari-internal IRI and is marked as such; it remains referenceable and queryable within the knowledge graph. Artwork individuals produced by ObliquER carry prov:wasGeneratedBy pointing to the viewsari:entity_linking activity that resolved them.

biographyc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001008

The life and works account of a single artist or craftsman as written by Vasari. Each biography is treated as a distinct fabio:Expression that is part of a volume expression, spans a contiguous range of printed pages, and contains a set of paragraphs. In Viewsari, biographies are the primary unit of discourse for coreference resolution: ObliquER does not draw coreference arcs across biography boundaries. Page ranges are derived from the printed page numbers of the Du Vere edition; the start page of a biography is the first #Page_N anchor in the Gutenberg HTML at which that biography's text begins.

expression c

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001010

The web-accessible manifestation of a single artist biography within a volume HTML file on Project Gutenberg. Because the Gutenberg edition hosts all biographies of a volume in one HTML file, the biography web representation is identified by the URL of the volume file combined with the #Page_N anchor of the biography's first page (e.g., https://…/25326-h.htm#Page_69 for Giotto's biography in Volume 1). As a subclass of fabio:WebPage, instances of this class carry an rdfs:seeAlso link to that anchor URL. The class serves as the range of frbr:isPartOf links from viewsari:page_web_representation instances.

coreferentc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001018

A mention that refers back to an entity already introduced earlier in the same biography, realised as a pronoun, demonstrative, definite noun phrase, or descriptive repetition (e.g., "it", "the work", "the said panel", "the same altarpiece"). Coreferent mentions do not introduce new entities; they extend existing coreference clusters established by prior explicit or implicit mentions within the same biography. Coreference is bounded by biography: arcs are never drawn across biography boundaries. Identity across biographies is established at the knowledge graph level via shared owl:sameAs links to external identifiers, not through coreferential annotation.

editionc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001000

A published version of a work, representing a distinct textual state produced by an editorial process. In Viewsari, the primary instance is the 1568 Giunti edition of Le Vite. As a subclass of fabio:Expression, an edition is an abstract intellectual entity independent of any physical or digital carrier; physical or digital realizations are represented as fabio:Manifestation individuals linked via frbr:hasEmbodiment. Editions may differ from one another in textual content, organization, and completeness; the 1568 edition of The Lives differs substantially from the 1550 edition (Torrentiniana) and is the basis for the Du Vere translation.

extracted contentc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001033

The common superclass for all content-layer individuals produced by computational extraction from the Vasari corpus. By extending prov:Entity, every instance of this class inherits the provenance vocabulary (prov:wasGeneratedBy, prov:wasDerivedFrom) and can be linked to the extraction activity that produced it. Specific entity types — viewsari:person, viewsari:artwork, viewsari:location, viewsari:organization, viewsari:cooccurrence — are subclasses of this class. The class supports uniform provenance queries across all entity types without requiring UNION clauses for each subclass separately. Use this class as a filter in SPARQL when querying for any extracted entity regardless of type.

generic mentionc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001019

A mention that refers to an entity type in general rather than to a specific individual. Generic mentions arise when Vasari speaks of artistic practice as a craft process (e.g., "works of painting" in the sense of the art of painting) rather than to a specific artwork. Generic mention instances are retained in the knowledge graph to support studies of Vasari's rhetorical register and the distinction between referential and predicative language in art-historical prose, but they are excluded from ObliquER precision and recall computation and do not generate viewsari:artwork individuals.
has super-classes
Annotation c, mention c

implicit artwork mentionc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001021

An implicit mention whose referent is a specific, recoverable viewsari:artwork individual described through a descriptive passage rather than a canonical title. This is the primary target of the ObliquER extraction pipeline, as implicit artwork mentions constitute the majority of artwork references in Vasari and are the hardest to link to external knowledge bases. Entity linking for implicit artwork mentions relies on contextual signals (artist identity, location, iconographic subject, medium) rather than surface-form similarity.

implicit mentionc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001016

A mention in which a specific, recoverable entity is described but not named through a canonical surface form. The entity must be uniquely identifiable from contextual evidence (commission, location, medium, iconographic subject) even without a formal title. This is the paradigmatic mention type for artworks in Vasari, where descriptive circumlocutions such as "a panel he executed for the high altar of S. Croce" constitute the primary referential mode. The principal distinguishing criterion is recoverability: the mention must refer to a unique concrete entity that can in principle be identified on the basis of the passage.

mentionc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001026

A surface-form reference to an entity in the Vasari corpus, extracted by the ObliquER pipeline or by the NER and coreference resolution step over the Index of Names. Every mention is typed simultaneously as oa:Annotation (carrying oa:hasTarget, oa:hasBodyValue, oa:hasSource) and as prov:Entity (carrying prov:wasGeneratedBy, prov:wasDerivedFrom), reflecting its dual role as a scholarly annotation anchored in the source text and as the output of a documented computational process. The four disjoint subclasses — viewsari:explicit_mention, viewsari:implicit_mention, viewsari:coreferent, and viewsari:generic_mention — classify the referential mode of the mention. Subclasses for artwork mentions (viewsari:explicit_artwork_mention, viewsari:implicit_artwork_mention) further specialize the entity type.

named entity recognitionc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001022

A prov:Activity subclass representing a single run of the named entity recognition and coreference resolution pipeline. In the first population stage, instances of this class correspond to runs of the Index-of-Names-based NER pipeline that produced viewsari:mention individuals from the paragraphs of the Du Vere translation. In the ObliquER stage, instances correspond to LLM inference runs that produced viewsari:explicit_mention, viewsari:implicit_mention, viewsari:coreferent, and viewsari:generic_mention individuals. Each activity instance carries prov:wasAssociatedWith pointing to the executing prov:SoftwareAgent (a Python script or an LLM identifier), prov:used pointing to the consumed paragraphs and index resources, and prov:startedAtTime / prov:endedAtTime timestamps where available.

personc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://viewsari.ise.fiz-karlsruhe.de/ontology/#0001013

A historical individual mentioned in the Vasari corpus — primarily artists, architects, sculptors, and patrons, but also popes, rulers, and other figures named in the biographies. Person individuals are derived from the Index of Names in each volume of the Du Vere translation, augmented by coreference resolution over the paragraph texts. Each person individual is globally unique across all ten volumes: the same canonical index name yields the same person IRI regardless of which volume the mention appears in. Where a Wikidata entry exists, owl:sameAs links to the QID. Person individuals carry prov:wasGeneratedBy pointing to the viewsari:coreference_reconciliation activity that resolved them.

Records the character length of the merged plain-text content of a doco:Paragraph as a non-negative integer. The value is computed after page-fragment merging and normalization and reflects the length of the string stored in viewsari:hasText. This property supports corpus-statistical queries (e.g., identifying paragraphs likely to contain multiple entity mentions by length threshold) and provides a fast validation check: the oa:end offset of any viewsari:position_selector contained within a paragraph must not exceed this value.
